Watch it run

See your code run one step at a time.

pytobs loops.py✕

─☐✕
What you see

Press F6 instead of Ctrl+R. pytobs records every line your program runs and what each variable holds at that moment. Step forward and back with the arrow keys: the editor highlights the line, the panel shows which variables changed, and lists and dicts are drawn as boxes with the loop's index under them.

What it teaches

Loops, variables, function calls, why a value is wrong. The thing courses explain with diagrams, drawn from your own code.

How it works

Python's built-in tracing (sys.settrace), recorded in your program's own process, capped at a few thousand steps so it stays instant.

Large · about a week

Explain this error

A plain-English note under every error, with a fix to try.

pytobs test.py✕
+
─☐✕
What you see

Under the traceback: what the error means in one or two sentences, then a corrected line built from your code. This one is your own TypeError from earlier. Works offline from a hand-written guide to the ~30 errors learners meet most.

What it teaches

Reading errors calmly instead of guessing. After a few weeks you won't need the note, which is the point.

How it works

A curated library of explanations matched on the exception type and message. No AI, no internet, instant.

Small · 2 to 3 days

Tests panel

Write small checks; see green and red.

pytobs count.py✕
+
─☐✕
What you see

Any function named test_… is a test. Ctrl+T runs them all and lists each one: green dot for pass, red for fail with expected vs got, and a link to the line. The habit that separates people who hope their code works from people who know.

What it teaches

Thinking about what code should do before and after you write it. Real programmers do this every day.

How it works

Uses pytest if it's in your .venv, and a small built-in runner when it isn't.

Medium · 3 to 4 days

Your progress

Streak, runs, time and the errors you meet most.

pytobs test.py✕
+
─☐✕
What you see

Ctrl+K → Progress. A 16-week activity grid like GitHub's, four big numbers, and your most frequent errors with the concept to review next to each. Everything stays on your computer.

What it teaches

Consistency, and knowing which concept is costing you the most time.

How it works

pytobs counts runs, errors and editing time in a small local file. Nothing leaves your machine.

Small · 2 days

My recommendation

Build them in this order

1Explain this errorFastest win; helps on every mistake from day one.
2Watch it runThe biggest learning tool here. Loops and functions click.
3Tests panelOnce your scripts have functions. A real-programmer habit.
4Your progressMotivation and a map of what to review.

About two and a half weeks for all four. Each ships on its own, so you can use the first one while the next is being built.

Mockups use example data. Real screens will show your own code, runs and errors.
